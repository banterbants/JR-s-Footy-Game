The game

  • Goal = 6 points — kick it between the two tall posts. Behind = 1 — either side of them.
  • Hold KICK to power up, aim with the stick, release to kick. The ring shows who you'll hit.
  • Mark it! Catch a kick of 15 m or more and you get a free kick. Run to the white ring and press MARK to fly.
  • Set shots: mark inside range and you can line up for goal — stop the needle in the green.
  • Don't get caught! Hang on too long and a tackle is holding the ball. Handball out of trouble.
  • On defence, press TACKLE next to the ball carrier, and SWITCH to your closest player.
